# Chromatic existence — stable Starscapes, White, Black and neon

**Status:** AUTHOR-ESTABLISHED working cosmology; fine color algebra and mathematical realization **OPEN**. Do not assert genuine proof from fictional definitions.

## 1. The six structural chromatic basins

The six basic structural energy identities are Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Blue, Violet. Their conventional reference positions in the normalized neon organization are:

| Basin | Reference |
|---|---:|
| Red | 1/7 ≈ 0.142857 |
| Orange | 2/7 ≈ 0.285714 |
| Yellow | 3/7 ≈ 0.428571 |
| Green | 4/7 ≈ 0.571429 |
| Blue | 5/7 ≈ 0.714286 |
| Violet | 6/7 ≈ 0.857143 |

Those are **reference equilibrium points**, NOT locked scalar intensities for every universe or immutable realized neon states. A specific Red contribution can be above or below 1/7 as that Starscape's valid equilibrium allows, analogously for every other basin. A basin color is not elemental fire, earth, air, or a magical school.

## 2. The Starscape is a stable White superposition

An individual **Starscape** realizes an admissible, stable White superposition of *its actual six chromatic neon configurations*. Different Starscapes may have different stable configuration sets. The configuration is not necessarily an evenly spaced reference ladder and not a universal identical white recipe.

Conceptual author expression:

```
ν = (νR, νO, νY, νG, νB, νV)  # actual admissible neon configurations
W(ν) = Superpose(νR, νO, νY, νG, νB, νV)
```

`Superpose` is a conceptual operator; its actual mathematical algebra is OPEN in `_bricked`. The six chromatic *realms* are associated with the individual dominant identities and White is the shared Material expression, but one must not conflate this with a planet/city/trade network. The realm is a cosmological environment, not every local planet in a single starscape.

## 3. Black is the complete neon spectrum

**BLACK** is the entire normalized neon spectrum `[0,1]`, across the chromatic organization, treated as **one unified identity**, not separate access to six colored buttons or an eighth chromatic basin. **Kira is the manifestation of Black. Full stop.**

```
Black — whole neon domain: [0,1]
White — stable superposition of six actual chromatic configurations
Kira  — conscious manifestation of Black
```

This establishes **identity**, not automatic mathematical infinity of energy delivered, perfect skill, unlimited realm control, instantaneous mastery or physical invulnerability. Realized access, throughput, knowledge, skill, sustainable operation and output are distinct. Kira's practical abilities come through living Black companion Bindings; nothing here gives her an arbitrary collection of spells.

Each names a fundamental operation and principal constraint of magic; together they provide the overarching mythic and structural framework of the seven-book saga. Describes what it means for a magical expression to be valid: physical inscriptions in `ru’ne`, architected sigils, speech by a prophet, conceptual statements, and Genesis Field semantic expression are relevant *examples*, not a canon rule that every incantation must be spoken or every spell written. ### VII. Cartographers — spatial adjacency

Altered geometric neighbors and admissible spatial connections, including nonordinary chromatic realm geometry. Connections to the Horizon Leviathan's moving continuum are **POSSIBLE EXAMPLES**, not an already proven Cartographers mechanism.

Specific wording, exact completeness, resulting effect on the Starscape, and whether the ancient vision was predictive or causally active **OPEN**.

# The magical engine — Transduction, `vaen`, Binding, Shaping

**Status:** MK147 inherited with author-selected MK231 refinements. Distinguish identity-based Binding from conscious active shaping. Avoid importing MK157's novel-specific economy or Valnak procedures.

## Six Transduction domains

1. **Persistent Topology / Coherence–Decoherence** — coherence persistence, reinforcement and controlled loss/alteration of coherence. Healing archetypes here, but not every magical effect is healing.
2. **Dimensional Geometry** — dimensional/manifold organization.
3. **Genesis Field Topology** — relation/topology and organizational domain interaction.
4. **Genesis Expression** — realized Genesis expressions, which may include traditional-looking fireballs and other manifestations when admissible.
5. **Resonance** — coupling, oscillatory organization and sustained interaction.
6. **Chirality** — directed occupancy/organization, admissibility, structural color relation.

**Six domains are NOT equal to six chromatic races, six colors, or first six prophecies.** R/O/Y/G/B/V are structural identities usable in domain-dependent combinations; they do not individually mean a universal set of elemental spells.

## `vaen` — lower-case canon spelling

`vaen` (V-A-E-N, lowercase) names naturally occurring identity-bearing specialty resources from wilds, dungeons, domai and other environments. These can be valuable in Binding Circles, Alchemy or related craft. They are not a synonym for every ordinary chemical element, mana crystal, or cash denomination. Existing MK147 examples include **Voidbloom Lotus** and **Teardrop of the Everlasting Star**. **Void flower lotus** as spoken may refer to this; until explicitly renamed, keep the MK147 spelling **Voidbloom Lotus**.

Scarcity and competition among alternative recipes make some vaen prohibitively expensive for ordinary families. Specific recipes, prices, harvesting monopolies and supply flows remain OPEN unless individually established.

Source directly verified: https://github.com/xApologies/Mk-147/blob/main/live-model/04_SYSTEMS/BINDING_VAEN.md

## `ru’ne` and sigils

A `ru’ne` is an orthogonal physically inscribed geometric mark/component; a **sigil** is a complete structure of ru’ne that needs a material substrate. Arrays may support infrastructure, water, sanitation, growth/industrial matter cultivation, lighting, barriers and stabilization. Their substrate/geometry maintenance matters. `velis` are temporary field anchors in inherited worldbuilding, distinct from durable physical sigils.

Source directly verified: https://github.com/xApologies/Mk-147/blob/main/live-model/04_SYSTEMS/SIGIL_ENGINEERING.md

## Binding Circle operation

A Binding Circle is a **large specialized identity-resolution sigil/scaffold** that selects admissible identities from identity-bearing inputs, resolves/collapses and occupies the target with the selected identity or state. Inputs may carry multiple identities, and Binding need not transfer the whole original object; targets may be people or objects. Vaen are important examples but **not the only possible source of identity**.

```
source identity (vaen or other compatible input)
    → Binding Circle / configured Genesis crystals / valid topology
    → admissible identity selection
    → collapse / resolution
    → target occupied identity / stored Binding architecture
    → subsequent conscious Shaping (when appropriate)
```

**Normal Binding** is not synonymous with an active fireball; a fireball, metal manipulation or water creation may be a resulting shaping. Ordinary Shaping may require PSSP projection, basin access, a deliberately understood concept, controlled manifestation and release.

## Six conscious shaping phases (author vocabulary)

`Conceive → Shape → Hold → Resolve → Manifest → Release` — working narrative description of conscious shaping, not a replacement for the separately persisted Binding structure. Use `shaping` as normal in-world technical term, with `spell` only as familiar explanatory gloss. Exact stored-architecture versus projected-manifold terminology remains open upstream.

## Fatigue, feasibility and training

- No global mana bar. Fatigue is physiological/endurance-like loss of a person's ability to sustain Genesis-field/basin access under complex conscious shaping.
- Training may improve skill, control, efficiency and endurance, but doesn't grant unlimited access or automatically change stored Binding identities.
- An effect requires suitable topology, compatible input, enough available bandwidth/energy delivery, continued attention and appropriate duration. Don't convert illustrative examples into rigid spell cooldown mechanics.
- Human White-native persons have broad access to Binding with suitable transduction resources, not guaranteed success or unrestricted skill.
- Chromatic native beings may develop identity-appropriate Bindings within their own intrinsic specialization, not an open library of any arbitrary category.
- Kira after the Black Phoenix Binding is an **exception**: she can no longer make ordinary Bindings, only future Bindings with Black existences. Her actual shaping/capability comes from those living relationships.

## Healing and the Affliction

Within this story the Affliction is a **genetic pathological condition**. A conventional therapeutic Binding recipe combining specific rare vaen with proper Genesis crystals and a prepared Binding Circle can treat it. These ingredients are expensive *because they are rare and useful for other purposes*, not automatically because the magical act of healing is uniquely difficult. Exact composition and price remain OPEN. Kira's family cannot afford the complete treatment despite many years of saving.

The Black Phoenix egg appears to be an unusual effective **vaen** to knowledgeable examiners because touching it temporarily pushes back the pathology locally in her hands. Their ordinary category judgment is understandable but incomplete: the egg is actually an identity-bearing future **living Phoenix**, and Kira's attempted therapeutic Binding becomes an extraordinary shared-existence Binding instead. The full biological healing outcome is OPEN.

2. **Organic Primordials** — powerful healing via biological/coherence expression.
3. **Coherence-specialist Dragons** — healers among the six individual Dragon specialization types.

The placement of unicorns or nonorganic primordials in a total ranking was not established; do not fill one by extrapolation. The preceding is a limited comparison, not absolute quality of life or racial worth.

# Native races — expressions, biology, forms and limits

The following is the accumulated native-race workshop state. The original v0.3.0 master in `history/` retains additional discussion and rejected/alternate visual nuances; the updates here reflect newest decisions.

## Red — Dragons

Extremely large living animals/beings, strong, long-lived, forceful and physically resilient. Across the dragon **population** there are **six single-domain specializations**, one for each Transduction operational domain; **a single dragon has one specialization**. Red's early reference is 1/7, often lower sustainable energetic output/shorter fatigue endurance than larger-scale magically robust beings, partly compensated by huge mass and mechanical strength. Different individuals may have very different physical and magical practices. `Dragonspeak` is a structured dragon-to-dragon language of communicated **thoughts/emotions/images**; its transmission, syntax and outsider access remain OPEN.

White-humanoid female reference: trim/athletic/toned, slight localized draconic scales (often crown) and **no large physical tail**, not generic curvaceous dragon woman in full body scales. Rejected early exaggerated silhouettes do not define the species. Black Dragon in Kira's saga develops from an **egg**; individual specialized category remains OPEN.

## Orange — Phoenixes

Phoenixes are magical avian beings with **fire, flight, light, exceptional Coherence healing and rebirth**. They are the top-established healer category. **Reproduction is nonbiological in the conventional sense: phoenixes combine energies to create offspring/eggs.** This post-v0.3.0 decision supersedes the old `reproduction unknown` line. Exact energy ingredient identities, developmental timing, rebirth memory and strict conservation laws remain OPEN. Electromagnetic/photon attacks are speculative direction, not locked weapons.

White-humanoid female reference: hair like fine feather filaments/plumage, subtle birdlike detail, independent personhood. The **Black Phoenix Egg** is an unprecedented energy-created egg with Black Flame potential that ordinary observers do not understand. Its creation prompts the unicorn to speak the prophecy; it is later stolen, lost, found by Kira and bound through shared existence before/at hatching. Black is not automatically a cosmetic pitch-black feather phenotype.

## Yellow — Primordials

Conscious conductors of matter, often physically composed of their own organized substance/process. Three broad category families, NOT exactly three races:

- **Inorganic:** stone, mineral, metal/alloy, crystal, organized inorganic lattice.
- **Organic:** biological plants, flowers, roots, fungi etc., second in the limited healer hierarchy through organic/coherence interactions.
- **Process:** ongoing organized processes such as combustion/fire, atmospheric interaction, fluid motion/plasma; exact water/air boundaries need refinement. Chemistry distinctions matter: fire is a process, H₂O a compound, air mixtures of materials.

Humanoid body is **matter/process organized in the human outline**, not merely a human cosplaying in an elemental outfit. Kira's Primordial companion begins from a **Genesis crystal-lattice cradle**, an egg analogue; its Black manifestation and individual identity are OPEN. Earlier locked Book V Yellow placement remains current while late possible switching with Blue is OPEN.

## Green — Kitsune

Sentient foxlike beings with cunning and playful cultural association. Advanced capability to **realize an admissible perceived potential into locally supported reality**, connected to Genesis Field Resolution, local Bandwidth, and Chirality. They are **not ghosts/spiritual beings**, and projection is not limited to mere visual illusion. A master may generate dreamlike realized environments; exact persistence/cost/conditions OPEN. Material-humanoid trait: fox ears; **one literal physical tail**, plus eight trailing Genesis/phase-state *projected echoes* when the tail moves. Not nine biological tails. Kira's Black Kitsune is expected to be an existing **conscious individual** and Binding requires a voluntary agreement; birth/emergence after first Phoenix Binding and exact form open.

## Blue — Leviathans

Colossal organisms, at least hundreds of meters in many candidates, differentiated by unusually great simultaneous Genesis **energy throughput**, large-scale spatial organization and sustained endurance. The energy-basin reference positions lie between zero and one. Red is **1/7**, Orange **2/7**, Yellow **3/7**, Green **4/7**, Blue **5/7**, Violet **6/7**.
