### 6. Living Art — biology and ecology

Magic may be grown, bred, grafted and cultivated. Organized magical expressions and spells **can be alive without being conscious**. The author connects this to the natural world, living magical structures and organic Primordials. Not all magic is automatically alive/sentient; not all organisms are spells. ### 7. Cartographers — geometry and spatial adjacency

The fantasy-facing geometric consistency domain, directly tied to Genesis Field mathematics/topology and permitted relationships of space. Includes nonordinary continuous geometry, valid adjacency, closed spatial structure, mapping and boundaries. It does not automatically give every character portal, teleportation, or unlimited spatial rewriting abilities; those must be separately defined. The original geometric constraint is binding.

# SEVEN-DOMAIN COSMOLOGY, STARS CAPE, CIVILIZATION AND COEXISTENCE

## A. Two vocabularies, one cosmological arrangement [AUTHOR-ESTABLISHED]

- **Narrative:** Seven Domains / Seven Realms. R, O, Y, G, B, V, plus White called the **Material Realm**.
- **Mathematical/authorial structural explanation:** The White **Material Resolution** is the superposed expression under Genesis Field resolution, bandwidth, chirality and the accepted cosmological arrangement. It is ordinary spacetime in the context of this story, not another independent single-basin region.
- **Starscape:** The universe-scale explorable environment with galaxies, stars, planets and moons. Not synonymous with one planet or star system; 'universe' is an author synonym but 'Starscape' preferred. MK147 does contain a **local circumbinary star system** (Kira's World, Ash World, Frontier World, Great Giant) as separate **planets**; these named worlds are *not* additional colored domains. Do not transplant local astronomical specifics to the new plot without decision.
- **Chromatic domain:** A native nonplanetary environment/existence associated with a dominant energy-basin structure. The realm color is a structural basin identifier, **not a promise the landscapes are that color**.

## B. The 80/20 domain composition rule [AUTHOR-ESTABLISHED]

The six chromatic realms have **80%** of their persistent energetic/structural support in their dominant basin, and **20%** arranged from the other five basins. For the reference diagram, the secondary 20% was visually spread equally (4% each). **80/20 is the committed structural rule**; whether the 20% must always distribute equally in all locations remains open. White is a balanced **superposition** with approximately 1/6 each from six basin constituents as a normalized visual composition; no seventh White independent energy basin exists.

| Realm | Dominant basin | Rest of scaffold | Native population |
|---|---|---|---|
| Red | 80% Red | 20% among Orange/Yellow/Green/Blue/Violet | Dragons |
| Orange | 80% Orange | 20% among Red/Yellow/Green/Blue/Violet | Phoenixes |
| Yellow | 80% Yellow | 20% among Red/Orange/Green/Blue/Violet | Primordials |
| Green | 80% Green | 20% among Red/Orange/Yellow/Blue/Violet | Kitsune |
| Blue | 80% Blue | 20% among Red/Orange/Yellow/Green/Violet | Leviathans |
| Violet | 80% Violet | 20% among Red/Orange/Yellow/Green/Blue | Unicorns |
| White (Material Resolution) | none | six-way superposition; reference weights ~16.67% each | Humanity; universal shared environment |

**Do not confuse the 80/20 composition weights with the following 1/7–6/7 energy scale.** They represent different aspects of the setting. Do not arbitrarily rewrite either as 1/6→6/6.

## C. Structural energy reference scale [AUTHOR-ESTABLISHED]

The energy-basin reference positions lie between zero and one. Red is **1/7**, Orange **2/7**, Yellow **3/7**, Green **4/7**, Blue **5/7**, Violet **6/7**. These are not damage multipliers, not six available spell classes, and not percentages of 80/20 composition. White does **not** automatically become 7/7 or a higher-energy seventh chromatic tier: it is the superposed material configuration.

At the native-population level: Dragons are relatively low energy, Phoenixes higher, Primordials intermediate, Kitsune Green, Leviathans Blue, unicorns highest chromatic availability. **Native energy availability is distinct from operational specialization, learned knowledge, instantaneous throughput, fine control, sustained endurance, and fatigue.** A Phoenix can be a better healer than a unicorn in one task, or a dragon more dangerous physically, without breaking this order. No quantitative power/damage/endurance equations beyond the stated structural references are canonized.

## F. Geography — nonordinary continuous environments [ACCEPTED VISUAL DIRECTION; EXACT TOPOLOGY OPEN]

The six chromatic realms should not be ordinary spherical planets. They are potentially unbounded or effectively unbounded continuous environments, with globally unfamiliar geometry while locally supporting recognizable cliffs, seas, forests, mountain terrain, sky, life and travel. **Nonordinary continuous geometry** is a possible characteristic of **all six**, not a unique Leviathan power or specific color element.

Illustrative domain imagery accepted for exploration (not geography maps or final physics):

- **Orange:** skyborne rolling seas/tundras of flame, through which Phoenixes can fly; surface areas below may exist. Orange is **not** defined as a uniformly orange-colored landscape or a fire-as-chemistry basin.
- **Blue:** skyborne oceans or water regions between which Leviathans can move; terrestrial and aerial niches may also exist. Enormous scale and nonordinary geometry, but we did not fix a literal ground/sky species taxonomy.
- **Violet:** immense cliffs flowing into nebula-like cosmic scenery; apparent astronomical distance may not equal intrinsic traversable distance. **No requirement for floating planets or sailing ships** despite an illustrative user-uploaded inspiration image. Unicorn use of chirality fabric/spatial traversal remains an OPEN possibility, not canonized teleportation.
- **Red/Yellow/Green:** geography not yet developed in comparable detail. Do not manufacture volcanic-red dragon continents, yellow-only crystal plains or obligatory green forests from the labels. Kitsune's *realized potential* can produce unusual local environments; do not identify all of Green's ambient geography as a Kitsune creation.

**Horizon Leviathan** is a special Blue native creature whose moving *persistent/vanishing continuum* may sustain an environment that follows it. This is not the whole Blue realm, not a world attached to its skin, and not a literal walking continent. See `03` and `05`.

## G. Human Material Realm civilization [MK147 INHERITED + AUTHOR DIRECTIONS]

The White Material Realm is the **universal playground/interchange** where seven populations meet. Humans cannot travel to a different chromatic realm on behalf of another chromatic population without additional mechanics, but can act as intermediaries/diplomats because humans themselves can naturally visit each.

Economics: inhabitants of Blue, Violet, Red and other chromatic realms cannot naturally travel directly into one another's domain. Cross-realm buying/selling, exchange of rare goods, negotiations, services and logistics are routed through common material commerce. Human jurisdictions can levy transactions **within their jurisdiction**, creating prosperity. This is a **symbiotic** ecosystem, not a human conquest empire, metaphysical human ownership of all transit, or exact tax percentage. The White cities also gain immense cultural integration.

Civilization inherits MK147's late-1600s / early-1700s romanticized preindustrial aesthetic with a divergent magical technological path. Infrastructure and built form are primarily **orthogonal**: cubic/rectangular stone grown inside bounded Genesis/sigil arrays, with Lego/Minecraft-like modular assembly. Curves/arches require distinct growth geometries or later shaping/carving; do not replace it with generic sweeping medieval fantasy arches. Sigil arrays support water, sanitation, lighting, plant growth, industrial matter cultivation and permanent durable architecture. Greenery, flowing water, vines, gardens and foliage soften straight built geometry. Architecture can endure centuries. There can be metalcraft, alchemy, clothing, sailing, carriages, etc., but not an automatic industrial-revolution progression. Already established MK147 civilization; **do not redesign** it for this series without Ink asking.

`_raeon` contributes Raeon, the in-world card game, as cultural inheritance. The game's rules belong to its source repository and should not be casually replaced.

## H. Biological and cultural coexistence [AUTHOR-ESTABLISHED]

**Humanoid manifestation** is a **Learned Cultural Competency** among chromatic peoples who have encountered humanity over vast periods. Visitors learn an appropriate humanoid biological manifestation and some of the shared language and customs, like learning a second language for travel; it is commonplace/propriety rather than a rare magical achievement. One need not look exactly human: distinctive ears, tail projections, small horns, crown scales, finely feather-like hair, material-based body composition and so on may reveal origin. White's environment is structurally favorable for humanoid morphologies; that does not imply all nonhuman native forms are categorically impossible there. A 300-foot dragon can have its native body yet human-scale form is vastly more practical in a city or Binding Circle. Native conscious/energy identity persists through manifestation.

Common language is learned for trade, diplomacy and ordinary interaction. Each population and region can retain its native language(s). The origin, label, grammar, written script and relationship to Genesis Language remain OPEN. **Dragonspeak** is an especially distinctive dragon-to-dragon language sending *thoughts, emotions and images* as structured communication; exact mechanics/range and outsiders' access OPEN. No automatically universal mind reading.

**One human species; three evolutionary biological expressions in White:** dwarven, ordinary human, elven. Dwarves: short/broad/muscular; familiar humans: varied general form; elves: tall/slender (author example up to ~7 ft), subtle tapered ears, unusually developed abstract cognitive capacities/neuroplasticity. These are **not three chromatic species or three different magically privileged races**. All share human White/Binder access. Human civilization is one integrated planetary society, not mandatorily three rival kingdoms. Elven cultures may lead advanced Genesis mathematics/sigil scholarship because of their neurological/knowledge differences, **not because elves have intrinsic higher-color magic**. Earlier speculative numerical IQ 'averages' were illustrative fiction author brainstorming, **not a binding quantified biological law**; no worth ranking of beings. Individual variation remains possible.

## C. Orange — Phoenixes [AUTHOR-ESTABLISHED plus OPEN light details]

## D. Yellow — Primordials [AUTHOR-ESTABLISHED threefold grouping]

Primordials are conscious **conductors of matter**—organized chemical, biological or process identities, potentially assuming humanoid configurations **made from their own substance/process**.

- **Inorganic:** metal (iron, copper, alloys), stone/mineral/silicate, crystalline carbon/silicon, crystal lattices. These include solid material and mineral beings, not simplistic rock spellcasters.
- **Organic:** living plant/tree/root/flower/fungal or other organic biological organization. Distinguished from all carbon chemicals having life; their morphology derives from their actual structure. **Second-ranked healer class** through coherence/topological reinforcement.
- **Process:** sustained reactions and systems including **combustion/fire**, plasma, organized fluid circulation and atmospheric processes (water/air/storm variants are candidates, not mandatory finalized subtypes). Fire is a *sustained chemical/physical process*, not a periodic-table element; H₂O is a molecular compound; gas/atmosphere mixtures are combinations of ordinary substances.

The 3×3 render visually used **inorganic** stone/metal/crystal; **organic** wood/floral/mycelial; **process** fire/water/storm. These are examples of possible manifestations, **not a final list of exactly nine species**. Organic healing second to Phoenix; inorganic/process healer ranking otherwise undefined.

High-level Kitsune could produce complex, dreamlike **realized environments/domains**, not merely fool sensory perception. Whether the structures persist independently, what gets imported into matter, local bandwidth cost, range, temporal coupling and what exactly is admissible remain OPEN. No forced multiverse, quantum interpretation, arbitrary time rewriting, or instant unlimited creation.

**Humanoid morphology:** recognizable fox ears. **Exactly one native/physical tail** in the adopted humanoid form; as that tail moves, **eight additional Genesis/phase-state projected tail traces** lag/trail its movement like ethereal afterimages. Total impression evokes nine without nine independent physical tails. The green Kitsune render is a **visual proposal**, not permission to introduce spirits despite its decorative rendered wording.
