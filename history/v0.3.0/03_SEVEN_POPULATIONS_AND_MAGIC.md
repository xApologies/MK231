## B. Red — Dragons [AUTHOR-ESTABLISHED]

Six distinct categories of dragon, one for each of:

1. Persistent Topology / Coherence–Decoherence (includes our Coherence Dragon healer classification)
2. Dimensional Geometry
3. Genesis Field Topology
4. Genesis Expression (can include fireball as a low-output shaping)
5. Resonance
6. Chirality

The **race** is a broad/generalist expression across these six types. **Individual** dragons are **specialized**, not assumed to use all six. They are comparatively low on the energy scale (1/7) and may fatigue earlier under sustained magical load; strength, physical mass, durability and longevity compensate. Imagine giant dragons, perhaps hundreds of feet long, not simply stupid brutes. A Red dragon fireball is lower output than a comparable higher-energy fireball under otherwise comparable conditions; no damage formulas are assigned.

Dragonspeak: shared/communicated **thoughts, emotions, images** as a dragon-native language. The way dragon consciousness and society use this is worth separate workshops; its range, encoding and comprehension by outsiders are unfinalized.

Material Realm female humanoid visual: human-proportioned body, **trim, toned, athletic/muscular rather than exaggerated voluptuous**, small localized scale accents (e.g. at crown), no permanent big fully scaled humanoid armor/skin; **no tail** for approved art direction. Possible subtle draconic crown/horns remain visually exploratory. No rule about all dragon sex distinctions, sexual cognition or multiple-domain overlaps has been accepted as biological canon. First rendered draft with large tails/voluptuous build was rejected and re-rendered.

## C. Orange — Phoenixes [AUTHOR-ESTABLISHED plus OPEN light details]

Phoenixes are avian magical beings whose expressions include **fire, flight, light**, outstanding **coherence/persistent topology stabilization** and healing, some **decoherence**, and **rebirth**. Their rebirth involves collapsing to ashes/residual energy and being reborn; underlying reproduction, memory, age and conservation rules OPEN. Phoenixes are the **number-one healing population**. Fire is richly expressed and Phoenix control can surpass a low-energy Genesis Expression Dragon's fireball.

**Electromagnetic/photon burst** potential was discussed as *50/50* exploratory: fire's visible glow involves electromagnetic radiation, but this is not an established phoenix ability to shoot particle beams/railguns. Light is among the accepted directions; fine mechanism OPEN. Phoenix flame expressions can appear across a rainbow without determining Phoenix cosmological origin (always Orange-native). **Black flame** exists as the central protagonist companion anomaly; its essence unknown.

Humanoid direction: fine featherlike hair/plumage, thin filaments draping like thick hair, possibly down back; avian traces without a stereotypical winged costumed human. Initial 3×3 render sheet is illustration only, not nine classes or sanctified costumes.

## D. Yellow — Primordials [AUTHOR-ESTABLISHED threefold grouping]

Primordials are conscious **conductors of matter**—organized chemical, biological or process identities, potentially assuming humanoid configurations **made from their own substance/process**.

- **Inorganic:** metal (iron, copper, alloys), stone/mineral/silicate, crystalline carbon/silicon, crystal lattices. These include solid material and mineral beings, not simplistic rock spellcasters.
- **Organic:** living plant/tree/root/flower/fungal or other organic biological organization. Distinguished from all carbon chemicals having life; their morphology derives from their actual structure. **Second-ranked healer class** through coherence/topological reinforcement.
- **Process:** sustained reactions and systems including **combustion/fire**, plasma, organized fluid circulation and atmospheric processes (water/air/storm variants are candidates, not mandatory finalized subtypes). Fire is a *sustained chemical/physical process*, not a periodic-table element; H₂O is a molecular compound; gas/atmosphere mixtures are combinations of ordinary substances.

The 3×3 render visually used **inorganic** stone/metal/crystal; **organic** wood/floral/mycelial; **process** fire/water/storm. These are examples of possible manifestations, **not a final list of exactly nine species**. Organic healing second to Phoenix; inorganic/process healer ranking otherwise undefined.

## E. Green — Kitsune [AUTHOR-ESTABLISHED; precise mechanics OPEN]

Foxlike intelligent beings; known fantasy signifiers include fox ears, mischievous/deceptive play and magical shifting. No spirits or spirit-energy explanations. Central magical identity is **making a perceived, admissible possibility locally realized**. Earlier phrase: 'Kitsune make the perception of reality real.' More precise author direction: *realize potential within Resolution and locally anchor it to the geometry's Bandwidth*, with advanced **Chirality** control. The Kitsune is the source of its resolution-related interaction; illusion/deception/shapeshift are expressions. High-level Kitsune could produce complex, dreamlike **realized environments/domains**, not merely fool sensory perception. Whether the structures persist independently, what gets imported into matter, local bandwidth cost, range, temporal coupling and what exactly is admissible remain OPEN. No forced multiverse, quantum interpretation, arbitrary time rewriting, or instant unlimited creation.

**Humanoid morphology:** recognizable fox ears. **Exactly one native/physical tail** in the adopted humanoid form; as that tail moves, **eight additional Genesis/phase-state projected tail traces** lag/trail its movement like ethereal afterimages. Total impression evokes nine without nine independent physical tails. The green Kitsune render is a **visual proposal**, not permission to introduce spirits despite its decorative rendered wording.

## F. Blue — Leviathans [AUTHOR-ESTABLISHED direction; one slot OPEN]

Leviathans can be immense (hundreds of meters or more) and are defined chiefly by **simultaneous volume/amount of Genesis energy controlled**, **long cognitive/endurance sustain**, and ability to establish **enormous spatial influence**. Their strengths are throughput and persistence, not necessarily precision/dexterity. Planetary-scale influence and ancient continental restructuring were imaginative upper-end proposals, **not a statement every Leviathan has universal tectonic control or a known historical event**. Blue is the 5/7 chromatic energy position, but the enormous throughput/endurance differentiates it from other populations beyond this reference point.

### Horizon Leviathan — special identity and image lock

Author treats it as the **apex/top-level** within proposed Leviathan power scaling. The creature is **not** itself a continent, terrain on its back, or an ordinary walking landmass. Its existence may sustain a **moving environment of geometric persistence**—'where it moves, a world follows.' This is also the **Moving Horizon**, **Persistent Continuum** and **Vanishing Continuum**, interpreted as one underlying phenomenon (maybe 'infinity as a closed domain', currently conceptual metaphor). Environmental realization/persistence may accompany the creature; trailing geometry may fade when local sustained organization ceases. How far the environment persists, whether it ceases to exist vs reverts to an underlying ordinary reality, exact operator, scale, closed-infinity mathematics, interaction with Kira, and changes on humanoid manifestation all **OPEN**. No assertion of an infinite physical body or omnipotence.

## G. Violet — Unicorns [AUTHOR-ESTABLISHED; DEFINITION FROZEN]

**Unicorns are the highest chromatic-native energy population (6/7) and are the masters of all Seven Foundations as ONE unified expression of magic.** This is the precise accepted identity. It must not be rewritten as 'all six transduction specialties', 'universal energy donor', 'generic all-magic recipient', or a list of specialized spells merely because another population is discussed. A Violet unicorn can be extremely powerful; precise workings across individual foundations remain OPEN and the seven original constraints still matter.

Violet's projected environment may present locally accessible nebula-like 'sky', cliffs and distances unlike ordinary 3D astronomy. Rainbow Road / Chirality Fabric connection to exotic traversal is an idea to investigate, not a proved transporter. No obligation to place ships or floating literal planets in its native domain.

Humanoid visual direction: exceptionally symmetrical, refined angular facial/bone structure, exotic beauty, slim/trim/lithe athletic physiques of varying height, not necessarily extremely tall or muscular. A **small shimmering/projected horn** is the intended in-world cue; the selected 3×3 rendered depiction may not get the horn exactly right and is a **description base, not definitive canon anatomical detail**.

## H. White — humanity [AUTHOR-ESTABLISHED]

Humans are **blank canvases** for magic: generalized White superposition permits a human to receive **Bindings from all six transduction domains**, limited by resources, Binding access, understanding, geometry and cognitive fatigue. No one human automatically performs every magical ability. Magic is accessed through mathematics, sigils, Binding Circles and Shaping; innate native chromatic magic does not belong to ordinary humans. Elven, dwarven and ordinary-human biological morphs remain **one human species**. Elven culture can be mathematically advanced due to cognitive model-building; being intelligent isn't magical energy output in itself.

**Nonhuman Binding restriction:** A chromatic race/being only accepts Bindings **compatible with its established native magical identity**. For individual dragons specifically, a Chirality Dragon accepts chirality-related Bindings but not an unrelated domain Binding. Other races' exact sets follow their unique native identities; **don't invent a full legal binding catalog for unicorns/Kitsune/etc.** The user specified that incompatible attempts consume supplied resources and energy bleeds into the ambient Genesis/Starscape field, without establishing a stable personal Binding. The precise matter/energy transformation accounting is not yet specified. These rules are **new series overlay**, NOT all verbatim MK147 existing behavior. Original MK147 also supports object targets and individual Binding incompatibility: do not erase that as a 'universal guaranteed success' rule.

Dragons and humans were described as having comparable **low-end magical expression** and a tradition/potential rivalry; no specific history of wars or conquest has been formally authored for this new series.
