## A. New series premise and inheritance boundary [AUTHOR-ESTABLISHED]

This is a **new** fantasy series, not a revision of the existing MK147 seven-book trajectory or MK157's Valnak plot. Inherit **MK147's existing mathematical/fantasy/world/cultural baseline unchanged where applicable**; intentionally use MK157's explicitly referenced Afflicted Kira's smithy/biological portrayal as an inspiration for the new Kira. ### The protagonist

**Kira**, human, Afflicted, brought up as a blacksmith/armorsmith (especially expert at chainmail/mail craft). The **chainmail robe** is a valued inherited character/craft motif, not yet given an exact role in this new plot. Ordinary humanity is blank-canvas Binding-compatible, but Kira becomes a structural exception through a special shared-existence Binding.

**Early Kira biological reference** (selectively inherited from MK157): pathological, chalky/calcified pale/white skin, brittle white hair, worn/sickly appearance; reaching 22 is unusual relative to MK157's rough prior Afflicted lifespan estimate (11–17 was a *working author model*, not a law). Existing MK157 post-transform reference has healthy white skin/hair, angular/symmetrical features and black/rainbow eyes, but **the new series has not yet committed to duplicating that entire eventual transformation**. For the new first contact, **only Kira's hands** visibly regain healthier white skin as she touches the egg. Do not silently give her MK157's original Black Armor or powers.

Kira's immediate motivation: **survive the Affliction**, not save seven civilizations or conquer anyone. An important human princess remains an intended central story figure (relationship to Kira, appearance, and role beyond princess OPEN). Earlier loose possibility of a unicorn/female companion is now encompassed by the explicit six-companion structure; character details are not locked.

## B. Prologue and Chapter One — current event scaffold [AUTHOR-ESTABLISHED]

**Prologue (author-side knowledge):** In the Orange/Phoenix Domain there exists a **black egg** foretold to realize the **Black Flame**; the black flame has **not** been realized while still an egg. Someone steals the egg. A chase / realm-to-realm or interplanetary bridge passage goes wrong, and the egg is lost from its homeland, emerging elsewhere in the White Starscape. Meanwhile others continue searching across the Phoenix realm and Starscape, ignorant of the new location. Villain identity, thief motive, detailed chase mechanics and nature of the prophecy/fate are OPEN.

**Chapter One (author event plan):** Kira is transporting armor from the Village Hub toward the early-region dungeon/support settlement **Hollowmere / voice-variant 'Holomir'**, on her home planet. The exact spelling, map label/site, alignment with MK147/MK157 and new book geography remain to be checked against the user-supplied map; MK157 currently includes **Hollowmere**, but it is not safe to assume the new series has definitively adopted that spelling/address. Along the journey Kira discovers a **large black egg roughly ostrich-egg-sized**, radiating perceptible Genesis energy. When she lifts it, the pathological calcification of her hands begins rapidly to improve: still white, but healthy. Kira perceives the egg might be an unusual Genesis `vaen` / identity-bearing resource useful for a Binding and potentially her recovery. She protects it and plans to gather other compatible resources and Genesis crystals for a Binding Circle.

**Binding event (still requiring mechanism design):** She deliberately attempts a Binding in hopes of healing herself. Unexpectedly, the egg's identity and Kira's existence enter an unusual **shared-existence relationship**. Author shorthand: **the egg binds to her** rather than functioning like a conventional target's passive ingredient. The egg hatches into an independent Black Phoenix. Their Binding is not a pet ownership contract or loss of the Phoenix's personhood. Why this event occurs, how identity selection functions for an unhatched conscious being, what mutual consent means here, and whether the first Binding heals all of Kira remain OPEN.

**Fate/black singularity working mystery:** Author envisions Kira as perhaps a 'Black Singularity', 'the other side of existence', a structural condition needed for the Black Flame to realize. This is a **deliberately mysterious author hypothesis**, not finalized MK Ultra ontology or a new unlimited power.

## C. The Diplomatic Pouch — Kira's shared-existence anchoring [AUTHOR-ESTABLISHED]

Nickname **the Diplomatic Pouch** arose from Kira's ability to keep companions together across otherwise incompatible realms.

A companion keeps its independent native identity, mind, agency and freedom; the special shared-existence Binding with Kira does **two** things:

1. **Anchored realm access:** The companion can naturally enter its own realm and White, and also any other chromatic realm **when Kira is there and stabilizes compatible local existence**. Kira must occupy the domain; if she leaves, the visitor cannot remain safely indefinitely (exact tolerance/unravel rate OPEN). Example: the Orange-native Phoenix can be in Orange, White, or Red **while Kira is present in Red**. This is not independent lifelong permission for the Phoenix to travel every realm.
2. **Shared abilities:** **Kira gains access to magical capabilities of her bound friends** through the relationship. The exact mechanism (does Kira independently shape, channel through companion, share a projected topology, require their active participation?) remains OPEN. This explicitly **supersedes** early suggestions that Kira would obtain *no* abilities. She still does **not** begin with a conventional personal catalogue of native offensive Bindings.

**Consent:** For the Kitsune and unicorn, Kira and the existing conscious other being **mutually consent** to an intentional friendship/relationship Binding. For egg-born companions and Primordial cradle, exact prehatching consent is OPEN and must be addressed rather than automatically given an invented solution. These are relationships among people, **not summoning non-sentient equipment, commandeering a mind, or treating friends as resources**.

Kira's literary power progression comes primarily from **friendship, companionship, voluntary collective capability and shared existence**, rather than a list of spell purchases. The seven conscious individuals ultimately form a group of Kira plus six independent magical beings. The group is not a 'harem', inventory system, six obedient pets, or standard summoning class unless the author later chooses specific relationship narratives.
