### 1. Seven Realms — existence under compatibility

Originally Six Realms, now explicitly renamed Seven Realms after White (Material Resolution / narrative Material Realm) was integrated. Six chromatic native domains and their White superposition explain compatibility of distinct native existences. Humans can naturally traverse all seven; nonhuman chromatic natives generally inhabit their native and White domain; Kira's shared-existence anchoring is the exception for her companions. Colors name dominant structural energy basins, **not elements or transduction specialties**. See `02` for 80/20 composition and independent structural scale.

### 2. The Unwritten — grammar and meaning

The ability to express admissible statements through meaningful structure. Language/semantic distinctions matter. This may be discussed alongside Genesis Language, physical ru’ne, and sigil inscriptions, but spoken words, semantic grammar, geometry and inscribed sigils must not be declared identical. The precise crosswalk is not completed; the original constraint **grammar and meaning** remains. User called it a description of how words, language and meaning are expressed.

### 3. Borrowed Hours — temporal obligations

Not exclusively clock-hours. Seconds, minutes, hours, centuries, millennia and potentially entire durations of existence may be in scope. The author connected the foundation to MK Ultra's Time Shell, local anchors, temporally localized bandwidth and resolution, and the possibility of accessing a prior configuration without ordinary physical backwards time travel. Example: reaching back five seconds to influence an outcome occurring three seconds later. That is an exploratory interpretation, **not** permission to change history freely, and not a proved temporal operator. The original function *transfer duration* and constraint *temporal obligations* remain. Explicit borrowing source, obligations and temporal conservation are OPEN.

### 4. Thousand Threads — relationships and connections

Existing relational structures connect realms, identities, meaningful expressions, time and material geometry. Core operation *modify relationships*, constraint *existing connections*. Author called this broadly underlying geometry/relations of reality. Distinct from Cartographers: relational connectedness and spatial adjacency are different categories, even where they interact. Exact admissible operations (create, sever, strengthen) and identity continuity remain OPEN.

### 5. Covenant Gardens — consent and obligation

Original independent premise concerned agreement/negotiation; the author later related it to how geometries and biological systems agree to coexist, as well as the obligations of life. Do not force this into literal conversations with mountains, ghost contracts or spirits: **no spirit ontology is approved**. For conscious beings (e.g., Kira/Kitsune/unicorn mutual contracts), consent has meaningful agency; for non-conscious systems, any geometric 'agreement' is only a structural metaphor until formally defined. Preserve both original operation and broader interpretation; no automatic contracts among all objects.

### 6. Living Art — biology and ecology

Magic may be grown, bred, grafted and cultivated. Organized magical expressions and spells **can be alive without being conscious**. The author connects this to the natural world, living magical structures and organic Primordials. Not all magic is automatically alive/sentient; not all organisms are spells. No new mana, life-force or spirit energy is added. The actual ecology, growth and reproduction rules remain OPEN.

### 7. Cartographers — geometry and spatial adjacency

The fantasy-facing geometric consistency domain, directly tied to Genesis Field mathematics/topology and permitted relationships of space. Includes nonordinary continuous geometry, valid adjacency, closed spatial structure, mapping and boundaries. It does not automatically give every character portal, teleportation, or unlimited spatial rewriting abilities; those must be separately defined. The original geometric constraint is binding.

## C. Existing MK147 Genesis machinery [VERIFIED INHERITANCE; author clarification where identified]

The human name **aera** refers to a culturally recognized expression of the Genesis reality interface. Transduction is the physical/geometric reorganization of energy through constraints and coherence. Do not introduce mana.

**Ru’ne:** discrete, strongly orthogonal, physically inscribed geometric components of a sigil. **Sigil:** complete operational physical structure realized on a substrate. **Sigil array:** coordinated sigils for infrastructure/material cultivation. Long life is tied mainly to persistent materials and geometry; examples include water, sanitation, lighting, barriers, industrial material growth and metropolitan stabilization. Sigils are not universally 'spoken spells'. [MK147 verified]

**Binding Circle:** a large specialized sigil/identity-resolution scaffold. Selects admissible identities from identity-bearing resources and resolves them into a target occupied identity/state (targets can include people and objects). `vaen` are unusual naturally occurring identity-bearing specialty resources, not automatically the only sources. Genesis crystals/diamonds serve as energy storage and supporting resources. [MK147 verified]

**Binding versus Shaping:** Binding creates the enduring working structure or access architecture. Active Shaping is separate. PSSP (phase-state shadow projection) is the projected/manifold structural interface. MK147 explicitly marks some nomenclature for the stored structure versus projected manifold **OPEN**; do not erase this provenance issue merely because the author has a conceptual shorthand.

**Complete active Shaping — author nomenclature:** `Conceive → Shape → Hold → Resolve → Manifest → Release`. The attached *Naming Conventions.pdf* specifies this six-stage process and distinguishes **Aspect** (persisting structured energetic configuration) from **Manifestation** (event). The author calls the **whole pipeline** a *Shaping*; `Shape` alone is one stage. MK147's current public summary does not fully document this six-stage naming, so track it as author clarification/supplementary source, **not an independently verified verbatim MK147 statement**.

**Generalized author visualization of active Binding use:** person bears localized binding topology (spiky-ball analogy); consciously conceives target; expands/projects a compatible structural scaffold; accesses required energy basins and loads organized energy; holds/stabilizes; consciously resolves/collapses structured energy into realization; manifests intended effect; releases constraints. The analogy is a teaching aid, **not** a literal ball, mandatory field geometry or new alternative six stages. Basin requirements depend on structural needs of each operation, not on an elemental color association.

**Cognitive fatigue — explicit author correction:** ability to consciously organize, access and sustain Genesis-field/basin interaction declines under load, like muscles fatigued while holding a heavy dumbbell. At exhaustion, the person *cannot* maintain energy-basin access, though the basins remain. This is not mana exhaustion, depletion of the universe, or a merely psychological belief. MK147 repository currently uses a broader *physiological/endurance-like* label; for this new workshop the author specifies cognitive mechanism. Keep the contrast documented rather than misquote the source.
