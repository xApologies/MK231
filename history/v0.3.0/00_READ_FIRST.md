### Decision status vocabulary

- **AUTHOR-ESTABLISHED:** Explicit decision/correction expressed by Ink in this conversation. This outranks assistant speculation, regardless of age.
- **VERIFIED INHERITANCE:** Directly supported by a checked repository/document; retain source-specific caveats and ownership.
- **WORKING / PROPOSED:** A favored but not finally resolved interpretation, design, example or hypothesis. May not be silently promoted to canon.
- **OPEN:** Question not decided, including exact mechanics of appealing speculative concepts.
- **SUPERSEDED / REJECTED:** Explicitly displaced by a later author decision; retained only for provenance.
- **VISUAL REFERENCE:** Artistic guidance, not exhaustive or literal biological/mechanical canon.
