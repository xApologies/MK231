## A. Prior decisions retained from v0.3.0 (not rewritten)

- Five-layer Stack: L0 mathematical foundation; L1 fantasy engine; L2 World; L3 characters; L4 narrative.
- Six chromatic structural basins and White superposition; 80/20 dominant/scaffold as previously described *realm composition*; baseline reference neon levels Red 1/7…Violet 6/7 are a **separate construct**.
- Seven native populations: Dragons Red, Phoenixes Orange, Primordials Yellow, Kitsune Green, Leviathans Blue, Unicorns Violet, Humanity White. Human/dwarf/elf biological variants belong to one species.
- Dragons collectively cover six Transduction operational specialties but individually specialize in one; Phoenixes top healers, Organic Primordials next, Coherence Dragon next, human Bindings thereafter in that limited comparison.
- Kitsune physically possess ONE tail plus EIGHT projected trailing echoes, not nine separate biological tails; advanced realization of admissible potential (no spirit-energy).
- Unicorns unify mastery of ALL Seven Foundations as one coherent native expression. Never downgrade or reassign to one foundational domain.
- White is cultural/economic transit and exchange environment; chromatic natives normally visit own+White, humans naturally can visit all seven with higher energy discomfort.
- Six Blue Leviathan types selected (original candidate #01,03,05,06,08,13); Worldback #10 rejected; Horizon native-form reference **image variation #8 Fractal Spine**, while conceptual Vanishing Continuum persists. Horizon's humanoid-domain effect OPEN.
- Seven-book order: Orange → Green → Red → Violet → Yellow → Blue → White. Original MK147 Violet-first and initial ascending Rainbow order superseded.
- Kira's Black Phoenix shared-existence Binding grants realm anchoring for companions **while Kira is there**, and access to certain companion abilities, not ownership of creatures or unconstrained import of spells.
