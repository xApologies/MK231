# The magical engine — Transduction, `vaen`, Binding, Shaping

**Status:** MK147 inherited with author-selected MK231 refinements. Distinguish identity-based Binding from conscious active shaping. Avoid importing MK157's novel-specific economy or Valnak procedures.

## Six Transduction domains

1. **Persistent Topology / Coherence–Decoherence** — coherence persistence, reinforcement and controlled loss/alteration of coherence. Healing archetypes here, but not every magical effect is healing.
2. **Dimensional Geometry** — dimensional/manifold organization.
3. **Genesis Field Topology** — relation/topology and organizational domain interaction.
4. **Genesis Expression** — realized Genesis expressions, which may include traditional-looking fireballs and other manifestations when admissible.
5. **Resonance** — coupling, oscillatory organization and sustained interaction.
6. **Chirality** — directed occupancy/organization, admissibility, structural color relation.

**Six domains are NOT equal to six chromatic races, six colors, or first six prophecies.** R/O/Y/G/B/V are structural identities usable in domain-dependent combinations; they do not individually mean a universal set of elemental spells.

## `vaen` — lower-case canon spelling

`vaen` (V-A-E-N, lowercase) names naturally occurring identity-bearing specialty resources from wilds, dungeons, domai and other environments. These can be valuable in Binding Circles, Alchemy or related craft. They are not a synonym for every ordinary chemical element, mana crystal, or cash denomination. Existing MK147 examples include **Voidbloom Lotus** and **Teardrop of the Everlasting Star**. **Void flower lotus** as spoken may refer to this; until explicitly renamed, keep the MK147 spelling **Voidbloom Lotus**.

Scarcity and competition among alternative recipes make some vaen prohibitively expensive for ordinary families. Specific recipes, prices, harvesting monopolies and supply flows remain OPEN unless individually established.

## `ru’ne` and sigils

A `ru’ne` is an orthogonal physically inscribed geometric mark/component; a **sigil** is a complete structure of ru’ne that needs a material substrate. Arrays may support infrastructure, water, sanitation, growth/industrial matter cultivation, lighting, barriers and stabilization. Their substrate/geometry maintenance matters. `velis` are temporary field anchors in inherited worldbuilding, distinct from durable physical sigils.

## Binding Circle operation

A Binding Circle is a **large specialized identity-resolution sigil/scaffold** that selects admissible identities from identity-bearing inputs, resolves/collapses and occupies the target with the selected identity or state. Inputs may carry multiple identities, and Binding need not transfer the whole original object; targets may be people or objects. Vaen are important examples but **not the only possible source of identity**.

```
source identity (vaen or other compatible input)
    → Binding Circle / configured Genesis crystals / valid topology
    → admissible identity selection
    → collapse / resolution
    → target occupied identity / stored Binding architecture
    → subsequent conscious Shaping (when appropriate)
```

**Normal Binding** is not synonymous with an active fireball; a fireball, metal manipulation or water creation may be a resulting shaping. Ordinary Shaping may require PSSP projection, basin access, a deliberately understood concept, controlled manifestation and release.

## Six conscious shaping phases (author vocabulary)

`Conceive → Shape → Hold → Resolve → Manifest → Release` — working narrative description of conscious shaping, not a replacement for the separately persisted Binding structure. Use `shaping` as normal in-world technical term, with `spell` only as familiar explanatory gloss. Exact stored-architecture versus projected-manifold terminology remains open upstream.

## Fatigue, feasibility and training

- No global mana bar. Fatigue is physiological/endurance-like loss of a person's ability to sustain Genesis-field/basin access under complex conscious shaping.
- Training may improve skill, control, efficiency and endurance, but doesn't grant unlimited access or automatically change stored Binding identities.
- An effect requires suitable topology, compatible input, enough available bandwidth/energy delivery, continued attention and appropriate duration. Don't convert illustrative examples into rigid spell cooldown mechanics.
- Human White-native persons have broad access to Binding with suitable transduction resources, not guaranteed success or unrestricted skill.
- Chromatic native beings may develop identity-appropriate Bindings within their own intrinsic specialization, not an open library of any arbitrary category.
- Kira after the Black Phoenix Binding is an **exception**: she can no longer make ordinary Bindings, only future Bindings with Black existences. Her actual shaping/capability comes from those living relationships.

## Healing and the Affliction

Within this story the Affliction is a **genetic pathological condition**. A conventional therapeutic Binding recipe combining specific rare vaen with proper Genesis crystals and a prepared Binding Circle can treat it. These ingredients are expensive *because they are rare and useful for other purposes*, not automatically because the magical act of healing is uniquely difficult. Exact composition and price remain OPEN. Kira's family cannot afford the complete treatment despite many years of saving.

The Black Phoenix egg appears to be an unusual effective **vaen** to knowledgeable examiners because touching it temporarily pushes back the pathology locally in her hands. Their ordinary category judgment is understandable but incomplete: the egg is actually an identity-bearing future **living Phoenix**, and Kira's attempted therapeutic Binding becomes an extraordinary shared-existence Binding instead. The full biological healing outcome is OPEN.

## Healer standing (established earlier)

1. **Phoenixes** — foremost coherence/healing population.
2. **Organic Primordials** — powerful healing via biological/coherence expression.
3. **Coherence-specialist Dragons** — healers among the six individual Dragon specialization types.
4. **Humans** — accomplished healers via appropriate Bindings, knowledge and training.

The placement of unicorns or nonorganic primordials in a total ranking was not established; do not fill one by extrapolation. The preceding is a limited comparison, not absolute quality of life or racial worth.

## Different mechanisms must not be merged

- **Standard Binding** = admissible identity collapse into target.
- **Ordinary Shaping** = conscious access/manifestation of bound capability.
- **Kira Black companion Binding** = exceptional shared existence between independent beings, permits shared capabilities, and acts as an anchor for cross-realm compatibility when she is present.
- **Black Phoenix egg** = not a disposable healing reagent, even if Kira and examiners initially misidentify it.
- **Realm crossing via Rainbow Road** = not proven just because `_bricked` uses Rainbow Road for information transfer; interrealm organism traversal remains separate design work.
