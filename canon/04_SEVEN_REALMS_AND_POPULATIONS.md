# Seven native populations and their chromatic realms

**Status:** Established broad cosmology / physiology, with source-specific exceptions labeled. The races are not the Seven Prophecies, each one does not monopolize a prophecy, and basin colors do not encode elemental magic.

## Overview

| Native realm | Basin reference | Native population | Characteristic magical or structural expression |
|---|---:|---|---|
| **Red** | 1/7 | **Dragons** | Colossal physical power and longevity; collective coverage of six **individual-specialist** Transduction categories |
| **Orange** | 2/7 | **Phoenixes** | Fire, light, flight, coherence/healing, rebirth; first Black egg |
| **Yellow** | 3/7 | **Primordials** | Conductors of matter; inorganic, organic and process groups |
| **Green** | 4/7 | **Kitsune** | Realization of perceived **admissible potential** through Genesis topology / Resolution / Bandwidth / Chirality |
| **Blue** | 5/7 | **Leviathans** | Extraordinary throughput, broad geometry/scale, sustained endurance; six selected classifications |
| **Violet** | 6/7 | **Unicorns** | Native integrated mastery of **all Seven Foundations** as one coherent magical expression; keepers of ancient prophecies |
| **White Material Realm** | actual chromatic superposition | **Humanity** | Broad compatible Binding access across six Transduction operations, not preloaded personal magic; includes dwarf/ordinary human/elf biological expressions |

**Chromatic realm structural composition:** earlier working presentation says ~80% dominant basin + 20% scaffold involving others; evenly split five × 4% is an illustration. This is different from the normalized neon *reference* positions, and neither determines a universal combat level. The Blue Leviathan's remarkable throughput is a population trait rather than proof that Blue energy has more joules than Violet by definition.

## Realm geometry

- Each of six chromatic environments may have unfamiliar global topology: continuous/unbounded-looking space, internally non-isometric spatial relations, floating oceans, unconventional horizons, yet locally familiar animals, ground, sky, cliffs, etc.
- White is the familiar human material realm and cross-race commerce/diplomacy meeting environment. Other native populations can visit White and **their own** realm by ordinary compatibility.
- Humans, being White-native, can traverse **all seven** subject to increasing environmental discomfort and limits in higher chromatic realms. Do not revert to early incorrect rule that all humans require an anchor or portal escort merely to be compatible.
- A Phoenix cannot naturally enter Red/Green/etc unaccompanied; after Kira's shared-existence Binding it can enter a realm where **Kira is physically present and supporting local compatibility**. It cannot safely stay indefinitely once she leaves (duration open).
- Technical portal/bridge arrangements, travel timing, routes and safe transport are OPEN; do not conflate realm access *compatibility* with instant teleportation.
- Different realms' times need not line up one-to-one. Exact relation across realm clocks remains OPEN.

## Society and White's economic role

White is a **symbiotic interchange**, not an empire over the other six. People trade, exchange rare materials and knowledge, settle disputes and encounter other native populations in human society. Human jurisdictions may levy normal local transaction taxes/fees, but no universal canonical rate or total human control over transportation exists. Cultural humanoid forms and interspecies shared-language fluency are learned competencies for long-standing relations; native identities remain intact.

## Humanity as one species

Human, dwarven and elven phenotypes are **three evolved biological expressions within humanity**, not three separate cosmological racial populations, and all are White-native. Dwarven average bodies shorter/broader/muscular; ordinary humans varied; elves generally taller/leaner with tapered ears and notable abstract cognition/neuroplasticity and math culture. Such variation is story-world descriptive rather than an objective statement of personal worth. Do not assign elves an innate Violet basin or special color magic on that basis.

## Magic/healing remains independent of hue

Each race's magic follows its own species organization, not the ordinal color name alone. For example Phoenixes are Orange-native yet their fire and healing belong to their particular magical structure; Red's basin is not automatically fire. A dragon specializing in Coherence can heal, but dragons of the other five specializations cannot automatically do everything that the coherence type does. A unicorn uses all Seven Foundations as one coherent whole, without thereby making Violet equal to all other realm populations.

## Human-scale manifestation in White

Chromatic natives may learn humanoid biological forms, speech, gestures and norms to travel and participate in White society. They need not conceal species identity perfectly: crown scales, featherlike hair, singular projected horns, material-built tissues, fox ears and one literal tail plus projected afterimages, and tailored Leviathan morphology remain possible. Ability is **learned cultural competency**; it is not 'a human cast a shapeshift on each race'. The actual living companion's consent and agency persist across forms.
