# The realms, White civilization, culture and geometry

**Status:** MK147-derived L2 culture/world infrastructure plus current cross-realm story directions. Refer to full original v0.3.0 master for further description and names; do not delete non-conflicting detail.

## Chromatic spaces

All six chromatic realms may use nonordinary *globally continuous* geometry rather than conventional Earth-like spherical planets. Local landscapes, seas, forests, cliffs, weather and travel paths can still be recognizable. Domains are structurally different by dominant chromatic identity and native species, but labels alone do not require monochromatic scenery or element-specific physics.

- **Orange:** Phoenix flight, potentially skyborne moving seas/firelandscapes; terrain and populations TBD. Phoenix reproduction by energy combination is a recent addition.
- **Green:** Kitsune habitats and their locally realized possible environments. Don't assume every Green landmark is fabricated by Kitsune.
- **Red:** Dragons' complex native terrain and settlement/open space; do not force all-Red lava continents.
- **Violet:** Extraordinary vast cliffs and apparent cosmic/nebular horizon geometry; physically sensible appearance may conceal nontrivial adjacency. No obligatory sailing ships in cosmic skies.
- **Yellow:** Primordial environments that support inorganic, organic, process manifestations, not necessarily a single yellow crystal world.
- **Blue:** Suspended sea/sky regions and spatial grandeur accommodate enormous Leviathans; neither all Blue geography nor every ocean originates from a Horizon Leviathan.
- **White:** Material Realm, central place of normal human civilization and cross-race exchange. The actual locations/realm travel mechanisms remain unspecified.

## MK147 societal inheritance

Author selected a romanticized late-1600s/early-1700s **preindustrial** human-material society whose magic yields distinctive infrastructure rather than an ordinary industrial revolution. Orthogonally grown rectangular/cubic stone, sigils, arrarys, modular construction, shaped water channels and greenery predominate. Arches/curves require different geometries/carving, not assumptions of standard medieval Gothic architecture.

- **`ru’ne`:** basic inscribed orthogonal units used to compose sigils.
- **Sigils and arrays:** enduring physically realized constructions for water/sewage, illumination, growth, material processing, stabilizing buildings, farming and barriers; maintenance repairs substrate damage.
- **`velis`:** temporary field anchors in the inherited world, distinct from durable physical arrays.
- **`vaen`:** unusual scarce identity-bearing natural specialty resources for Bindings, Alchemy or other uses.
- **`raeon`:** in-world culturally important physical card game, rules governed by `_raeon`; glass/maege-glass cards remain compatible earlier cultural detail.
- **`helkir`:** healer vocation.
- **`aithren`:** adventuring/delving professional culture; institutions require source-appropriate interpretation.
- **`domai`:** temporary bounded Genesis collapse/storm event landscape; red-to-violet, non-conscious White crystal core in MK147 inheritance; *not* a seventh/White domai.

## White realm as a diplomatic/commercial interchange

Chromatic-native visitors generally visit White and their own native realm; other chromatic settings require special means. Humans can visit all chromatic realms with differing comfort/compatibility. Thus White-centered commerce helps connect cultures that cannot normally visit one another freely. Human governments can earn fees/commerce revenue within human jurisdiction, but humans are NOT metaphysical owners of all races/portals.

## Humanoid culture and language

Long-standing White integration teaches chromatic people a human-scale body form and shared language, akin to bilingual acculturation, without erasing native biological/psychological identity. Dragon native communication `Dragonspeak` may convey structured images/emotion/thought, not mind-reading magic. The origins of cross-population common tongue, exact scripts and fluent adoption timelines remain OPEN.
