**Status:** The framework/names/core semantic operations are AUTHOR-ESTABLISHED. Actual prophetic **verses**, unicorn speaker identity, dates, rituals, interpretations, and hierarchy of keepers are OPEN. The earlier screenshot read "Six Realms" and "Covenant Garden"; later author corrections explicitly replace those with **The Seven Realms** and **Covenant Gardens**. `Borrowed Hours` is the maintained canonical term; occasional spoken "Bard Hours" has not been adopted as a rename.

## Framing: Prophecies of magic itself

These are the **Prophecies of Magic**, not seven power classes, seven incompatible kinds of spellcasting, or seven laws confined *inside* the Seven Realms. Each names a fundamental operation and principal constraint of magic; together they provide the overarching mythic and structural framework of the seven-book saga. Six chromatic species, human White, their regions, civilization and Bindings are expressions within this more fundamental reality. **One foundation ≠ one native race**; all can participate in multiple foundations in distinct ways.

### I. The Seven Realms — EXISTENCE FIRST

Six structural chromatic reference basins R/O/Y/G/B/V plus the stable White superposition. The existence structure is more fundamental than the seven named realm environments; magic falls under existence rather than vice versa. The author will later define what magic ultimately is “for.” Do not prematurely bind this prophecy to a literal map or planetary count.

### II. The Unwritten — grammar and meaning

Describes what it means for a magical expression to be valid: physical inscriptions in `ru’ne`, architected sigils, speech by a prophet, conceptual statements, and Genesis Field semantic expression are relevant *examples*, not a canon rule that every incantation must be spoken or every spell written. Syntax/semantics, context and realization need further author development.

### III. Borrowed Hours — duration and obligation

Temporality, commitments sustained over time, transfer/borrowing of duration; potential upstream ties to Time Shell exist but are **not** fully worked. The author has not yet defined debt collection, loan conversion, contracts, time dilation factors or mechanics. Do not make them up.

### IV. Thousand Threads — meaningful relationships

Relations and connections between existing identities. Shared-existence Binding is a good **narrative interpretation** but not a definitive mathematical identity for the prophecy. It will have applications well outside the main characters.

### V. Covenant Gardens — consent and obligation

Magic pertaining to agreement, consent, negotiated relations, and consequent obligations. Useful when thinking about living companion consent and interrealm diplomacy, but has not been identified as a mandatory spell system that turns friendship into a legal contract.

### VI. Living Art — magic expressed through life

Biological organization, living beings, ecology, cultivation. Relevant to Phoenix rebirth, genetics/Affliction, Organic Primordials, cultures, adaptations and growth, without assuming each belongs exclusively to this prophecy.

### VII. Cartographers — spatial adjacency

Altered geometric neighbors and admissible spatial connections, including nonordinary chromatic realm geometry. Connections to the Horizon Leviathan's moving continuum are **POSSIBLE EXAMPLES**, not an already proven Cartographers mechanism.

### VIII. The Black Prophecy — union, not an eighth basin

A powerful ancient unicorn speaks a prophecy associated with the creation of the impossible Black Phoenix egg. Much later Kira's extraordinary Binding activates the unfolding cosmological Black manifestations. She is the manifestation of Black's complete `[0,1]` neon organization, becomes limited to subsequent Bindings with Black existences, and unites six independent native Black expressions through shared-existence relationships. Their completed union is the prophesied full manifestation of magic. Specific wording, exact completeness, resulting effect on the Starscape, and whether the ancient vision was predictive or causally active **OPEN**.

## Narrative rule

The Eight Prophecies are a **cross-book throughline**, not an instruction to give each book one different prophecy. The first seven describe fundamentals of magic; the eighth concerns Kira's unusual existence and the union of all six chromatic races around the White-native protagonist. Unicorns are keepers of this ancient lore; they may not understand it fully. The seven books follow Orange → Green → Red → Violet → Yellow → Blue → White.
