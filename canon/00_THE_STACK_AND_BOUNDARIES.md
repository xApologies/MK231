## Five sets of terms that must remain separate

1. **Six basins:** R/O/Y/G/B/V structural chromatic energy identities. Reference neon values 1/7…6/7 are *landmarks*, not permanent realized basin values; not fire/water/etc.
2. **White / Black:** White superposes six actual neon configurations into a stable Starscape; Black denotes the full normalized neon spectrum across `[0,1]`, not an eighth ordinary basin. Both can be discussed via an inherited *working* Möbius-like organization, with exact algebra OPEN.
3. **Seven realm expressions:** Six chromatic realms and the White Material Realm; seven native racial populations. These are *expressions of the structure of existence*; they do not bound the ontology of magic.
4. **Six Transduction domains:** Persistent Topology / Coherence–Decoherence, Dimensional Geometry, Genesis Field Topology, Genesis Expression, Resonance, Chirality. They describe operational magic, not chromatic racial colors.
5. **Eight Prophecies of Magic:** seven named Foundations/Prophecies describing magic's operations and constraints, plus the Black Prophecy foretelling Kira's exceptional sixfold union. Their real ancient prophetic texts are as yet unwritten.

## Existing Shaping model (do not oversimplify)

The author has used **Conceive → Shape → Hold → Resolve → Manifest → Release** for a six-operation conscious shaping arc. Binding is a distinct prior act establishing a durable identity architecture. Genesis Field/identity organization involves proper compatibility, spatial topology, phase-state shadow projection (PSSP), basin energy access, and conscious operator action. No 'mana bar'; effort is finite ability to sustain coherent Genesis-field/basin access, like endurance. Formulas for energy accounting and state-space are OPEN.
