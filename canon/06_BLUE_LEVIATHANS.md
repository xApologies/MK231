### The six current chosen types

1. **Abyssal Crown (#01):** A vast, deep-environment radial center with branching, influence-extending appendages. Original species appearance/specific ecological adaptations OPEN.
2. **Tidevault (#03):** Enormous broad/flattish aquatic/shelf-like body with distributed surface area, endurance and stabilizing mass. Does not literally have to carry buildings.
3. **Skyweaver (#05):** Immense thin ribbon or membrane creating expansive skyborne geometry, connections and movement.
4. **Stormmantle (#06):** Layered sail-like morphology governing great atmospheric interaction over time.
5. **Cloudbreaker (#08):** Heavily powerful aerial leviathan with muscle mass, wings/large surfaces and immense momentum.
6. **Horizon Leviathan (#13):** Potential apex of Blue's Leviathan classifications; supports a moving geometrically persistent domain. **SELECTED NATIVE-FORM VISUAL: Variation #8 `Fractal Spine`.**

## Horizon Leviathan — persistent/vanishing moving continuum

Author formulation: **“Where it moves, a world follows.”** It is not a literal continent, walking mountain, or animal with villages perched atop its skin. The creature remains a coherent organism distinct from the environment it sustains.

**Moving Horizon = Persistent Continuum = Vanishing Continuum** are three descriptions of *the same* proposed phenomenon, not three new native subspecies. Author analogy: infinity as a closed domain; the concept is metaphysical/worldbuilding intuition pending strict mathematical definition. As it traverses Blue's unusual landscape, local land/sea/sky geometry may become sustained in its presence; regions behind may fade as their sustained organization ceases. What physically remains after the Leviathan leaves is explicitly OPEN (genuine vanishing vs reverting to underlying ordinary topology). So are duration, causal direction, energy supply and any exact correspondence to Cartographers/Genesis closure.

**Apex language** is a directional power-scaling/worldbuilding note, not a final comparative scalar equation against all unicorns or an unbounded energy law.
