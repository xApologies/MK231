# Book I / Orange — opening event scaffold

**Status:** A mixture of established events and explicitly proposed editorial structure. DO NOT treat as finished chapters. Neither the Black prophecy verse nor the actual prologue dialogue exists yet.

## A. Earliest sign, subject to realm-relative time

**ESTABLISHED:** Phoenixes reproduce by combining energies. An unprecedented Black Egg is created in their Orange-native realm. Its existence is an anomaly connected to a Black Flame that should not normally be possible; an ancient powerful unicorn speaks the Black Prophecy. Unicorns become/persist as keepers of that ancient knowledge. The egg is then stolen and, during an escape or theft event, lost; it eventually reaches the human White Material setting. The actual length of time between creation, ancient prophecy, theft and Kira's arrival is OPEN because the realm clocks are not necessarily synchronized.

**PROPOSED:** Open the novel with the unicorn speaking the Black Prophecy, leaving the speaker and audience unidentified or only partially known. This may be a prologue; Ink said “maybe that's the prologue.” No prophecy wording is accepted yet.

## B. The mortal starting point

**ESTABLISHED:** Kira age 23, adopted Afflicted armorsmith/blacksmith raised in an economically modest village-hub family. Typical Afflicted deaths 11–17; oldest known survivor 37. Her household has spent years saving for a rare identity-based healing Binding but cannot obtain the final expensive ingredients. Society's superstitious contagiousness stigma isolates her. She has no ordinary professional Bindings because the family prioritizes survival over flashy smith upgrades.

## C. Discovery on delivery

**ESTABLISHED DIRECTION:** During an armorsmith delivery near the starting region's support settlement/dungeon route (Hollowmere or name variant still to verify), Kira encounters a strange black Phoenix egg roughly ostrich-sized. It seems to bear unusual Genesis power. Touch causes temporary localized improvement to chalky/pathological hand skin, revealing healthier white tissue during contact. This is a signal of possible therapy, not an already established cure.

## D. Consult and decision

**ESTABLISHED:** People familiar with vaen treat the egg as an extraordinary unknown one-off resource and affirm that the proper Binding Circle and Genesis crystals should be capable of resolving a treatment. Kira sees an achievable alternative to the impossibly expensive standard Affliction recipe. Name/location of consultant, how many additional ingredients she can afford, and any family objections OPEN.

## E. Attempted therapeutic Binding

**ESTABLISHED:** Kira voluntarily uses the Circle hoping to resolve an Affliction treatment. Instead the Phoenix's identity and Kira become a persistent **shared existence**. The Black Egg hatches as a free independent Black Phoenix. The Circle's ordinary vaen-selection model is not discarded; the rare identity source turns out to be more than expected. The actual consequences for her genetic illness, including whether she is immediately healed, remain OPEN.

## F. Second initiation of the prophecy

**ESTABLISHED:** Her Binding precipitates the larger cross-realm Black phenomenon. Kira manifests Black `[0,1]` and can no longer acquire ordinary Binding shapings. She must establish further Bindings with other independently existing/emerging Black beings to gain more capabilities. Across differing realm-relative clocks, further Black births/revelations begin. Certain parties may begin connecting anomalous events to the ancient unicorn prophecy; whom, when, and what they believe are OPEN.

## G. Immediate dramatic craft considerations — NOT CANON

PROPOSED only: make the financial stake concrete through a family ledger or visibly unfinished collection of rare vaen; allow a brief moment of healthy skin during egg contact to validate Kira's impulse; establish that an ordinary healer has examined her genetic condition; contrast the old unicorn prophecy's grand language with Kira's simple need to live. None of these particular scenes are written or mandatory.
