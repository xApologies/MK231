# Kira — Age 23, Affliction, family, and the price of survival

**Status:** Series-specific **AUTHOR-ESTABLISHED** narrative setup, overriding unchosen MK147/MK157 version details. Do not import MK157's Black Armor transformation into MK231.

## Core person

- **Name:** Kira.
- **Species:** White-native human; adopted into a village-hub blacksmith/armorsmith family. Her skill with forging/mail and armorsmithing is inherited direction; whether mail robe returns as a signature motif OPEN.
- **Age at opening:** **23** — explicitly revised from earlier discussed 22. Other age options (e.g. 37 for Kira herself) were floated and not adopted.
- **Condition:** genetically rooted **Affliction** causing pathological chalky/calcified skin, brittle white hair, impaired healthy body function and progressive failure; the exact biomedical genetics are fictional and OPEN.
- **Typical Afflicted mortality:** between **11 and 17** years of age (working common mortality window, not a precise universal hazard curve or an absolute kill switch).
- **Oldest documented survivor:** **37** years, explicitly accepted. This is a record, NOT biological maximum; Kira has survived unusually long at 23 but some hope remains.
- **Social stigma:** Afflicted people are treated as outcasts; superstitious people fear 'catching' the Affliction, which contradicts its established genetic basis. Her isolation affects opportunities and who would donate help.

## Family and economic position

Kira was **adopted** into an ordinary loving but low-income blacksmithing household in a village hub. The father and brother(s) work family craft; a younger sister is mentioned. Exact number of older brothers (earlier two; later author offered maybe one), individual names, ages, and backstory of adoption remain OPEN in the new series. Don't silently transplant MK157's Maelor/Naira/Caelen/Vaedren/Laina household without an explicit author decision. Source familiarity can help draft proposals but not lock names.

Kira and family spend years **saving every spare resource** toward an established therapeutic Binding. They are close to the cost but still cannot obtain the complete rare recipe; proximity is not enough without the actual final vaen. Their effort reflects continuing family hope, even though her lifespan is uncertain. This **explains why Kira has never purchased useful blacksmithing or martial shapings**, despite living in a magically advanced culture. Resources spent on enhancement would be resources unavailable to save her life.

Societal charity is limited. Wealthy people rarely dedicate a king's ransom of scarce vaen to an outcast with shortened prognosis. This is an economic/social pressure in the story, not proof all citizens are cruel or every possible benefactor must reject her. The family’s hope is rational because there is a documented 37-year Afflicted survivor.

## Therapeutic Binding: what the family is trying to buy

An **established expensive Binding recipe** can treat the genetic Affliction. The recipe requires scarce specific identity-bearing **`vaen`**, supporting Genesis crystals, and appropriately configured Binding Circle. Examples of vaen that exist in MK147 include Voidbloom Lotus / Teardrop of the Everlasting Star but **the specific Affliction recipe ingredients have NOT been selected**; do NOT assign them as required without author approval. Scarcity and alternative economic uses of ingredients explain the cost.

## Contact with the Black Egg

During an armorsmith delivery trip near the home village hub / dungeon support region, Kira discovers a strange **Black Phoenix egg** roughly the size of an ostrich egg in the working opening. While she holds it, the visible calcified skin of her hands temporarily recedes and reveals comparatively healthy white skin. That effect is **local and temporary during contact**, not established permanent recovery. For Kira this appears to be a miraculous clue: something in this unusual resource could replace an unobtainable healing ingredient or accomplish the desired Binding at lower cost.

She has it examined. People familiar with vaen regard it as a one-off unusual **identity-bearing vaen**, and judge that with appropriate Genesis crystals and properly configured Circle, it **should** permit an Affliction-curing Binding. They do not know this is the unprecedented future independent Black Phoenix or that a prophecy has begun. The egg's medical relief is real enough to support Kira's choice; the category interpretation is incomplete.

## The therapeutic attempt becomes extraordinary

Kira intentionally attempts an identity Binding **to fix the Affliction**, not to become a cosmic ruler. The Circle does not resolve an ordinary healing shaping. Rather, an exceptional **shared-existence Binding** joins Kira with the identity of the egg-born Phoenix. The egg hatches and the Black Phoenix emerges **independent and conscious**. What happens to the Affliction in the moment and over subsequent scenes is **OPEN**, apart from the prior temporary hand relief.

**After the first Black Phoenix Binding** she cannot make other conventional shapings/Bindings. Future Bindings can be made **only with Black existences**, and her growing capabilities come through six living companion relationships. She still requires knowledge, control and available energetic support: being the manifestation of Black does not confer omnipotent skill.

## Storytelling significance

Kira’s intention is survival; a rare object that appears to solve the family's exhausting financial problem becomes the catalyst for an ancient prophecy and a cosmological-scale chain of Black appearances. Neither Kira nor her family need know the prophecy. Her already-survived 23 years versus the 11–17 typical window and 37 documented record sustains dramatic tension without assuming she must die tomorrow. Keep her dignity, practical smith competence, and independent choices central.
